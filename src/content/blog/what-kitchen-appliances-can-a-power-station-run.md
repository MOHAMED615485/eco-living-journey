Kitchen appliances are the high-wattage, short-duration loads. A microwave or kettle draws 1,000-1,500W, which sounds like trouble, but you only run it for a few minutes. What matters is **watt-hours per use**.

<p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:#555;"><em>How the numbers work: wattages are typical ranges from manufacturer spec sheets and efficiency labels, so check the label on your own appliance. Runtimes are calculated, not lab-tested: usable energy = rated Wh &times; 0.85, divided by average watts.</em></p>


## 📊 Watt-Hours per Use

Typical wattages and run times. Check your own appliance's label.

| Appliance | Typical watts | Typical use | Energy per use | Uses per 1,000Wh (85% usable) |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--:|:--:|
| Microwave | 1,000W | 5 minutes | about 83Wh | about 10 |
| Coffee maker | 900W | 10 minutes | about 150Wh | about 5 |
| Electric kettle | 1,500W | 4 minutes | about 100Wh | about 8 |
| Toaster | 1,000W | 3 minutes | about 50Wh | about 17 |
| Slow cooker | 200W | 8 hours | about 1,600Wh | 0.5 |
| Induction cooktop | 1,800W | 15 minutes | about 450Wh | about 2 |

**Takeaways:** quick-heat appliances are fine on a modest battery, slow cookers eat a full day's budget, and an induction cooktop is only worth running for a few short meals.

## 🔥 Cooking Safely in an Outage

- **Never use a charcoal grill, camp stove or gas oven indoors.** The CDC warns these produce carbon monoxide.
- **Stagger heavy loads.** Run the microwave, then the kettle, not both together.
- **Keep the fridge first.** Reserve capacity for the fridge before the coffee maker. See [how long a battery runs a fridge](/blog/how-many-watts-does-a-refrigerator-use/).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a power station run a microwave?
Yes, if its output is above the microwave's wattage (typically about 1,000W). A 5-minute run uses roughly 83Wh, so a 1,000Wh class unit gives about 10 uses.

### How many watts does a coffee maker use?
About 900W while heating. A 10-minute brew is roughly 150Wh.

### Can I run an electric kettle on a battery?
Yes with a unit rated above 1,500W continuous. A 4-minute boil is roughly 100Wh.

### Can a power station run an induction cooktop?
Only a larger unit with output above about 1,800W, and only for short meals, since 15 minutes of cooking uses about 450Wh.

### Is it safe to cook indoors with a grill during an outage?
No. Charcoal grills, camp stoves and gas ovens used indoors produce carbon monoxide, according to the CDC.
