## What was the Bluetti AC200L?

The AC200L was a large portable power station in Bluetti's home-backup range, built around a roughly 2,048Wh battery and a 2,400W AC output. It was aimed at people who wanted to run a refrigerator, a chest freezer and a few small devices through an outage, rather than just charge phones.

## What replaces it: the Bluetti Elite 200 V2

The Elite 200 V2 sits in the same capacity tier. These figures come from Bluetti's published specifications:

| Continuous AC output | 2,600W |
| Resistive-load capability | up to 3,900W (no motor surge rating published) |
| Solar input | up to 1,000W |
| Weight | 53.4 lb |
| Battery life | 6,000+ cycles |
| Warranty | 5 years |
| Expandable | No |

## What this capacity can run, in calculated hours

These are planning figures, not test results. They assume the average draws we use across this site (45W for a typical chest freezer, 90W for a freezer plus refrigerator) and a 15% battery buffer.

| Load | Average draw | Approximate runtime on 2,073.6Wh |
|---|---|---|
| Efficient chest freezer | 30W | about 59 hours |
| Typical chest freezer | 45W | about 39 hours |
| Freezer plus refrigerator | 90W | about 20 hours |

Real runtime depends on room temperature, how often the lid opens and how full the freezer is.

## Before you buy any large power station

- **Check your appliance's startup surge.** Motor loads such as freezers and well pumps can draw several times their running watts for a moment. The Elite 200 V2 does not publish a motor-surge rating, so confirm your appliance's startup draw first. Our guide to [surge vs running watts](/blog/surge-vs-running-watts/) explains how.
- **Weight matters.** At 53.4 lb this is a unit you wheel or lift rarely, not a camping carry.
- **It does not expand.** If you may want more capacity later, compare it with an expandable unit in [EcoFlow vs Bluetti](/blog/ecoflow-vs-bluetti/).
