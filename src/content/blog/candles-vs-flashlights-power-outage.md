**Use flashlights or battery-powered lights, not candles.** Fire safety agencies, including the CPSC and the US Fire Administration, advise battery-powered lighting during an outage because an open flame in a dark, stressful house is a fire risk.

Runtimes are calculated, not lab-tested: usable energy = rated Wh &times; 0.85, divided by average watts.</em></p>


## ⚡ Quick Answer

- **First choice:** flashlights, headlamps and battery or rechargeable LED lanterns.
- **If you must use candles:** never leave them unattended, keep them away from anything that burns, and use sturdy holders on a stable surface.
- **Keep kids and pets away** from any flame.
- **Blow them out** before sleeping or leaving a room.
- **Store spare batteries** and recharge lights when power is on.

## 💡 Why Flashlights Win

A candle can be knocked over in the dark, ignited by drafts blowing curtains or paper, or left burning when you fall asleep. A flashlight has none of those risks, and modern LED lights are bright and run for many hours per charge.

## 🧰 Basic Outage Lighting Kit

1. One flashlight or headlamp per person.
2. Two or three LED lanterns for shared rooms.
3. Spare batteries, or a power bank to recharge USB lights.
4. A battery or hand-crank radio for alerts.

Our [72-hour survival guide](/blog/72-hour-power-outage-survival-guide/) and [best emergency kit guide](/blog/best-emergency-kit-power-outage-2026/) cover the rest of the kit. ## ✅ Bottom Line

Battery lights are safer, cheaper to run than you think, and easier to use in the dark. Keep candles as a last resort, supervised.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Are candles safe during a power outage?
Fire safety agencies recommend flashlights and battery-powered lights instead. If you use candles, never leave them unattended, keep them away from flammable items and use stable holders.

### What is the safest light in a power outage?
Flashlights, headlamps and battery or rechargeable LED lanterns, since they have no open flame.

### How long can a power station run LED lights?
About 5 W per lantern is typical. Five lanterns (25 W) would run roughly 35 hours on a 1,024Wh unit at 85% usable capacity, as a calculated estimate.

### Do I need spare batteries?
Yes. Keep spare batteries for flashlights and radios, or a charged power bank for USB-rechargeable lights.

### Should I use a gas lantern indoors?
Fuel-burning lanterns create fire and carbon monoxide risks indoors. See our carbon monoxide guide before using any fuel-burning device.
