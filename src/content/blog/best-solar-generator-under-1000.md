## ⏱️ Calculated Runtimes

Usable energy is 85% of capacity (inverter losses). Runtime is usable energy divided by average watts.

| Load (average draw) | Jackery 1000 V2 (~910Wh) | EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus (~870Wh) | Bluetti EB3A (~230Wh) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chest freezer, 45W | about 20 hours | about 19 hours | about 5 hours |
| Refrigerator, 60W | about 15 hours | about 14.5 hours | about 3.8 hours |
| Fridge + router + lights, 90W | about 10 hours | about 9.7 hours | about 2.5 hours |
| CPAP, 30W | about 30 hours | about 29 hours | about 7.6 hours |

Real average draw depends on your appliance, the room temperature and how often the door opens. A plug-in watt meter gives the best number. To check whether a unit will start your freezer, multiply its LRA by 120 and add 20%; see our [surge vs running watts guide](/blog/surge-vs-running-watts/).

## 3 Things That Matter More Than Price

### 1. Surge rating and continuous output, not just capacity

Watt-hours tell you how long a unit runs. Surge and continuous watts tell you what it can start. A freezer or fridge compressor briefly draws several times its running watts at startup, so check the appliance's LRA &times; 120, add 20%, and compare to the surge rating.

### 2. Battery chemistry

All three picks use LiFePO4, which is generally the longer-lived chemistry for a unit that will sit and cycle for years. Confirm the chemistry and the rated cycle life on the manufacturer's page before buying any unit.

### 3. Solar input capacity

For outages longer than a day, input capacity decides how much you can win back each day. Using panel watts &times; peak sun hours &times; 0.75, 400W of panels with 5 peak sun hours makes about 1,500Wh, more than a full charge of a 1,000Wh unit. Clouds and winter sun cut that sharply.

## What $1,000 Cannot Buy

A sub-$1,000 power station will not run central AC, an electric water heater, an electric dryer, or most 240V well pumps. For those you need a generator on a transfer switch installed by a licensed electrician. See the [home backup power guide](/blog/best-solar-generator-home-backup-2026/) for larger setups.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best solar generator under $1000 for home backup?**
For one freezer or fridge, the Jackery Explorer 1000 V2 is a simple, light choice, and the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus is better if you want faster recharging and expandability. Both run a typical chest freezer for roughly 19 to 20 hours at a 45W average draw.

**Can a solar generator under $1000 run a refrigerator?**
Usually yes. A fridge averaging 60W runs about 15 hours on the Jackery and about 14.5 hours on the EcoFlow. Check your fridge's LRA &times; 120 plus 20% against the unit's surge rating.

**Is 1000Wh enough for home backup power?**
For essentials such as a refrigerator, router, phone charging and LED lights, a 1,000Wh unit gives roughly 10 to 20 hours depending on the load. For longer coverage you need solar recharging or a larger or expandable unit.

**What is the difference between Wh and W in a solar generator?**
Wh (watt-hours) is capacity, the total energy the battery holds. W (watts) is output, the most it can deliver at once. Both matter: a large battery with a weak inverter may not start a compressor.

**How does the Jackery 1000 V2 compare to the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus?**
The Jackery is lighter at 23.8 lb and has slightly more capacity. The EcoFlow has higher output (1,800W vs 1,500W), a higher surge rating (3,600W vs 3,000W), about 56-minute wall charging versus about 1.6 hours, and can be expanded.

**How long does a solar generator battery last?**
LiFePO4 units are typically rated for thousands of charge cycles. EcoFlow rates the DELTA 3 Plus for 4,000 cycles to 80% capacity. Check each manufacturer's page for its figure and warranty.
