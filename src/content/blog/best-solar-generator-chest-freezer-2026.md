## Why Surge Matters for a Chest Freezer

Every chest freezer compressor has two power numbers. Running watts are what the freezer draws while it holds temperature. Startup surge is the burst of power needed for a fraction of a second when the compressor starts, typically 6-10 times the running watts.

If a generator cannot deliver that surge, it trips, and you may not find out until morning when the food is warm. To find your number, read the **LRA** (Locked Rotor Amps) on the data plate on the back of your freezer and multiply it by 120. For example, an LRA of 8.3 means 8.3 x 120 = 996W of startup surge. [Full LRA explanation here](/blog/what-is-lra-on-a-freezer/).

## How Long Each Unit Runs a Chest Freezer

A chest freezer's compressor cycles on and off, so what matters is its average draw over a full day, not its running watts. These figures use three average draws and a 15% battery buffer. They are calculated estimates, not measurements, and they match the numbers on our [chest freezer wattage chart](/blog/how-many-watts-chest-freezer/).

| Scenario | Avg draw | EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus | Jackery Explorer 1000 V2 | Bluetti Elite 200 V2 |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Efficient modern freezer | 30W | 29 hours | 30 hours | 59 hours |
| Typical freezer | 45W | 19 hours | 20 hours | 39 hours |
| Older freezer or hot garage | 65W | 13 hours | 14 hours | 27 hours |
| Typical freezer plus a fridge (about 90W combined) | 90W | 10 hours | 10 hours | 20 hours |

A typical outage under about 18 hours is covered by either 1,000Wh unit for a single freezer. For longer outages, a fridge on the same unit, or no solar, the Bluetti's larger battery is the safer choice.

## When None of These Is the Right Answer

If you are protecting a small chest freezer through short outages of a few hours, none of these units is necessary. A 500Wh-class station runs a typical freezer (45W average draw) for roughly 9 hours with the 15% buffer, which covers most short suburban outages. Buy a bigger unit when you are protecting several hundred dollars of food through multi-day outages, or running the freezer alongside other appliances. Otherwise you are paying for capacity you will not use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What size solar generator do I need for a chest freezer?**
For most chest freezers you need at least 1,000Wh of battery capacity and a surge rating above your freezer's startup surge. Find the LRA on the data plate, multiply by 120, and choose a generator whose surge or continuous output exceeds that number. All three units above clear every common chest freezer size.

**Can a 1000W solar generator run a chest freezer?**
Yes, if its surge rating is above the freezer's startup surge. A 1,000Wh unit runs a typical chest freezer (about 45W average draw) for roughly 20 hours, with a 15% battery buffer. The surge rating matters more than the watt-hours, so make sure it exceeds your freezer's LRA x 120.

**How long will a solar generator run a chest freezer during a power outage?**
On calculated estimates for a typical freezer, about 19 hours on the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus, 20 hours on the Jackery Explorer 1000 V2 and 39 hours on the Bluetti Elite 200 V2. Older freezers or a hot garage can cut those by a third or more.

**Will my solar generator hurt my chest freezer?**
No. These units produce pure sine wave AC power, which is safe for chest freezers and other motor loads.
