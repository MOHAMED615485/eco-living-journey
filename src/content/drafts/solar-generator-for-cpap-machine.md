## The One Thing That Matters Most: Pure Sine Wave

Before capacity. Before price. Before brand.

**Your solar generator must output pure sine wave AC power.**

CPAP machines — along with oxygen concentrators, nebulizers, and most medical devices — contain sensitive motors and electronics that require clean, smooth AC power. Pure sine wave output matches the power quality of your home's wall outlets exactly.

Modified sine wave output is a cheaper approximation that works fine for simple resistive loads like lights and phone chargers. But it creates harmonic distortion that can damage CPAP motors, void warranties, and in some cases cause the device to shut down mid-use.

**How to check:** Look in the generator's spec sheet for "Output Waveform." It must say "Pure Sine Wave." If it says "Modified Sine Wave" or does not specify — do not buy it for CPAP use.

Every generator on this list outputs pure sine wave. All major brands (EcoFlow, Jackery, Bluetti) use pure sine wave inverters.

## How Much Power Does a CPAP Actually Use?

This is where most guides get it wrong. They give you a single number. CPAP power draw varies significantly based on pressure setting and humidifier use.

**CPAP without humidifier:**
- Low pressure (6-8 cm H2O): 15-30W
- Medium pressure (10-12 cm H2O): 30-50W
- High pressure (15-20 cm H2O): 50-80W

**CPAP with heated humidifier:**
- Low-medium pressure: 80-130W
- Medium-high pressure: 120-180W

**BiPAP (higher pressure, dual setting):**
- Without humidifier: 40-80W
- With humidifier: 100-200W

The humidifier is the critical variable. A CPAP running without humidifier overnight uses approximately 250-400Wh. The same machine with humidifier on high uses 800-1,440Wh.

**Check your device settings.** If humidifier is set to level 3-5, you are drawing 3-4x more power than CPAP-without-humidifier estimates suggest.

## CPAP-Specific Tips for Power Outages

**Tip 1 — Turn off the humidifier during outages.** This single change reduces power draw by 65-75% and can triple your runtime. Modern CPAP humidifiers have a setting to disable them. Find it before you need it.

**Tip 2 — Reduce pressure setting if medically possible.** Discuss with your doctor whether a temporary lower pressure setting during outages is acceptable. Each cm H2O reduction saves approximately 5-10W.

**Tip 3 — Use DC power if available.** Most modern CPAPs have a 12V DC input (the cigarette lighter style connector). Running directly from 12V DC instead of through the inverter eliminates inverter losses (10-15%) and extends runtime proportionally.

**Tip 4 — Keep the generator charged.** Set a phone reminder to check the generator charge monthly. LiFePO4 batteries are fine stored at any charge level, but a generator at 20% when the power goes out is not helpful.

**Tip 5 — Test before you need it.** Plug your CPAP into the generator, turn off the wall breaker, and run it for a full night before a real outage. You want to discover any compatibility issues on a normal night, not during a hurricane.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What solar generator is best for a CPAP machine?
The Jackery Explorer 1000 V2 is the best solar generator for most CPAP users — pure sine wave output, 1,070Wh capacity for 22+ hours without humidifier, LiFePO4 battery for long-term reliability, and 11.8kg weight for portability. For users who also need to run a refrigerator or oxygen concentrator, the Bluetti AC200L provides more capacity.

### Can you run a CPAP on a solar generator?
Yes, provided the generator outputs pure sine wave AC power. All major solar generator brands (EcoFlow, Jackery, Bluetti) output pure sine wave. Avoid modified sine wave generators — they can damage CPAP motors and void warranties. A 500Wh generator handles CPAP-only overnight use without humidifier.

### How long will a solar generator run a CPAP?
A CPAP without humidifier (40W) runs approximately 22-24 hours on a 1,000Wh generator. With humidifier (120W), runtime drops to 7-8 hours on the same battery. The humidifier is the critical variable — turning it off during outages triples your runtime.

### Does a CPAP need pure sine wave power?
Yes. CPAP machines contain motors and sensitive electronics that require pure sine wave AC power — the same quality as your home's wall outlets. Modified sine wave power creates harmonic distortion that can damage the motor, cause shutdown during use, and void the manufacturer's warranty.

### How many watts does a CPAP use?
A CPAP without humidifier draws 15-80W depending on pressure setting — average approximately 30-50W. With heated humidifier, draw increases to 80-200W. At pressure 10 without humidifier, most CPAPs draw approximately 35-45W in real use.

### Can I use a CPAP without power during an outage?
Not without a battery source. CPAPs require AC or DC power to operate. Options include: a solar generator with pure sine wave output, a dedicated CPAP battery pack (smaller and lighter than a solar generator), or a 12V car battery with the CPAP's DC adapter cable. The DC adapter option eliminates inverter losses and extends runtime.
