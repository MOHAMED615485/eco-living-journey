## The One Thing That Matters Most: Pure Sine Wave

Before capacity. Before price. Before brand.

**Your solar generator must output pure sine wave AC power.**

CPAP machines contain sensitive motors and electronics that require clean, smooth AC power. Pure sine wave output matches exactly what comes out of your home wall outlet.

Modified sine wave is a cheaper approximation that works fine for lights and phone chargers. For CPAP machines it creates electrical stress that can damage the motor, void your warranty, and in some cases cause immediate shutdown.

**How to check:** Look in the generator's spec sheet for "Output Waveform." It must say "Pure Sine Wave." If it says "Modified Sine Wave" or does not specify — do not use it for CPAP.

Every generator recommended on this page outputs pure sine wave.

## How Much Power Does a CPAP Actually Use?

Most guides give you one number. The reality is more complicated.

**CPAP without humidifier:**
- Low pressure (6-8 cm): 15-30W
- Medium pressure (10-12 cm): 30-50W
- High pressure (15-20 cm): 50-80W

**CPAP with heated humidifier:**
- Low-medium pressure: 80-130W
- Medium-high pressure: 120-180W

The humidifier is everything. A CPAP without humidifier uses 250-400Wh overnight. The same machine with humidifier on high uses 800-1,440Wh — nearly 4x more.

**Check your device settings before buying a generator.** If humidifier is on level 3-5, you need significantly more capacity than most buying guides suggest.

## 5 CPAP Tips for Power Outages

**1. Turn off the humidifier.** Reduces power draw 65-75% and triples runtime. Find this setting before you need it.

**2. Use DC power if available.** Most modern CPAPs have a 12V DC input. Running direct from DC eliminates inverter losses and extends runtime 10-15%.

**3. Test before you need it.** Run your CPAP from the generator for a full night before a real outage. Discover any issues on a normal night.

**4. Keep the generator charged.** Set a monthly reminder to check battery level. LiFePO4 is fine at any charge level but you want it ready when the grid goes down.

**5. Check insurance coverage.** Some plans cover portable battery backup for CPAP users with a letter of medical necessity. Ask your sleep specialist — the Jackery 1000 V2 at $700-800 may be partially covered.

### Can you run a CPAP on a solar generator?
Yes, provided the generator outputs pure sine wave AC power. All major solar generator brands (EcoFlow, Jackery, Bluetti) output pure sine wave. Never use a modified sine wave generator for CPAP — it can damage the motor and void the warranty.

### How long will a solar generator run a CPAP?

### Does a CPAP need pure sine wave power?
Yes. CPAP machines require pure sine wave AC power — the same quality as your home wall outlet. Modified sine wave generators create harmonic distortion that can damage CPAP motors, cause shutdown during use, and void the manufacturer warranty.

A CPAP without humidifier draws 15-80W depending on pressure setting — average approximately 38W at pressure 10. With heated humidifier the draw increases to 80-180W. The humidifier is the critical variable.
