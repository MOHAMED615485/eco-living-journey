Staying connected is one of the cheapest things to back up. A router and modem draw around **20W together**, phones add a little, and a laptop about **45W**. Even a small power station keeps them going for a day or more, while a TV is the load that adds up fastest.

<p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:#555;"><em>How the numbers work: wattages are typical ranges from manufacturer spec sheets and efficiency labels, so check the label on your own appliance. Runtimes are calculated, not lab-tested: usable energy = rated Wh &times; 0.85, divided by average watts.</em></p>


## ⚡ Typical Draw

| Device | Typical watts |
|:--|:--:|
| Wi-Fi router | about 10W |
| Modem or fiber terminal | about 10W |
| Phone charging | about 10-18W |
| Laptop | about 45W |
| 55-inch LED TV | about 80W |
| Monitor | about 30W |

## ⚠️ When the Internet Is Down Anyway

A powered router is useless if the neighborhood's cable or fiber network has lost power. Plan for that:

- **Use your phone as a hotspot.** Cell service often keeps working at least for a while, though towers can lose power in long outages, and a charged phone with a power station behind it is the simplest fallback.
- **Download offline maps, music and documents** before a storm.
- **Charge power banks** whenever the grid is up.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How many watts does a Wi-Fi router use?
Typically around 10W, and a modem or fiber terminal adds roughly another 10W. Check the label on your power adapter for the exact rating.

### How long will a portable power station run my router?
At about 20W for a router plus modem, a 1,000Wh class unit runs them for roughly 40 hours, and a small 268Wh unit for roughly 11 hours (calculated, using 85% usable capacity).

### Can I work from home during a power outage on battery power?
Yes, for a laptop, monitor, router and modem. A load of about 105W runs for roughly 8 to 9 hours on a 1,000Wh class unit, if your internet connection is still up.

### Will my internet work if my router has power but the neighborhood is out?
Not always. If the cable or fiber network lost power, your router has nothing to connect to. A phone hotspot is the usual fallback.

### How much power does a TV use?
A 55-inch LED TV uses roughly 80W. Three hours of viewing is about 240Wh.
