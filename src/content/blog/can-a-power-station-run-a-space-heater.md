Short answer: a power station can run a space heater, but **not for long.** A standard space heater draws up to **1,500W**, so even a 1,000Wh class battery lasts well under an hour. If your goal is staying warm through a winter outage, an **electric blanket (about 100-200W)** gives far more warmth per watt-hour.

<p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:#555;"><em>How the numbers work: wattages are typical ranges from manufacturer spec sheets and efficiency labels, so check the label on your own appliance. Runtimes are calculated, not lab-tested: usable energy = rated Wh &times; 0.85, divided by average watts.</em></p>


## ⚡ The Numbers

- **Space heater (high):** up to 1,500W. US plug-in heaters are typically capped at 1,500W because of the 15-amp household circuit.
- **Space heater (low):** about 750W.
- **Electric blanket:** about 100-200W.

**What this means:** a 1,000Wh class unit gives about 35 to 40 minutes of a full-power heater. The same battery runs a blanket for most of a night. Heating one person is far cheaper than heating a room.

## ⚠️ Watch the Output Rating

A 1,500W heater on a power station with a 1,500W continuous rating (like the Jackery Explorer 1000 V2) leaves no headroom, so it may trip or run unreliably. The EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus (1,800W) and Bluetti Elite 200 V2 (2,600W) have more margin. Resistive loads like heaters do not need a motor surge, but they do need a high continuous rating.

## 🔥 Safety Rules for Winter Outages

- **Never use a gas stove, oven, charcoal grill or camp stove to heat your home.** The CDC warns these produce carbon monoxide.
- **Never run a generator inside or in an attached garage.** Keep fuel-burning generators more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents.
- **Install battery-powered CO alarms** near sleeping areas.
- **Keep space heaters away from flammables** and never leave them running unattended.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a portable power station run a space heater?
Yes, if its continuous output rating is above the heater's wattage, but a 1,500W heater drains most power stations in well under an hour. A 1,000Wh class unit gives about 35 to 40 minutes of full power.

### How many watts does a space heater use?
Up to 1,500W on high, which is the usual cap for plug-in heaters on a 15-amp circuit, and about 750W on low.

### Is an electric blanket better than a space heater on battery power?
For one person, yes. An electric blanket draws about 100-200W, so it can run for most of a night on a 1,000Wh class unit, while a 1,500W heater cannot.

### How long will a power station run an electric blanket?
At 200W, roughly 4 to 5 hours on a 1,000Wh class unit; at 100W, roughly 9 hours (calculated using 85% usable capacity).

### Is it safe to use a gas stove for heat during an outage?
No. The CDC warns that gas stoves, ovens, grills and camp stoves produce carbon monoxide and should never be used to heat a home.
