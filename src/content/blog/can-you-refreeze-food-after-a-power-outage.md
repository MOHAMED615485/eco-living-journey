**Sometimes yes.** USDA guidance says frozen food that still contains ice crystals, or is still at 40°F or below, can be safely refrozen, though quality may suffer. Food that has been above 40°F for more than 2 hours should be thrown out. Never taste food to decide whether it is safe.

## ⚡ Quick Answer

- **Refrigerator:** keep the door closed. Food stays safe for about 4 hours without power, according to USDA.
- **Full freezer:** holds its temperature for about 48 hours if the door stays closed. A half-full freezer holds for about 24 hours.
- **Refreeze** food that still has ice crystals or is at 40°F or below.
- **Throw out** perishable food (meat, poultry, fish, eggs, leftovers, cut produce, dairy) that has been above 40°F for 2 hours or more.
- **Use a thermometer.** Color and smell are not reliable. When in doubt, throw it out.

## 🌡️ The Rules in Plain Terms

| Situation | What USDA guidance says |
|:--|:--|
| Fridge, door closed | Safe about 4 hours |
| Full freezer, door closed | About 48 hours |
| Half-full freezer, door closed | About 24 hours |
| Food above 40°F for 2+ hours | Discard perishables |
| Food still has ice crystals or ≤ 40°F | May be refrozen |

Each time you open the door, you lose cold, so plan what you need before opening it.

## 🧊 Before and During an Outage

1. Put an appliance thermometer in the fridge and freezer now, so you can check temperatures later.
2. Keep the freezer full where you can. Full freezers hold cold longer. See [chest vs upright freezers](/blog/chest-freezer-vs-upright-freezer-power-outage/).
3. Group food together and keep the doors shut.
4. Use a cooler with ice for a fridge if the outage will pass 4 hours.

When power returns, see [what to do when power comes back on](/blog/what-to-do-when-power-comes-back-on/).

## ✅ Bottom Line

Check temperature, not appearance. Refreeze what still has ice crystals or is at 40°F or below; discard perishables left above 40°F for 2 hours or more. Official USDA guidance lives at fsis.usda.gov.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I refreeze food that thawed during a power outage?
According to USDA guidance, food that still contains ice crystals or is at 40°F or below can be safely refrozen, though quality may suffer. Perishable food above 40°F for 2 hours or more should be thrown out.

### How long does food last in a fridge without power?
About 4 hours if the door stays closed, according to USDA.

### How long does a freezer stay frozen without power?
About 48 hours if full, or about 24 hours if half full, with the door kept closed.

### Can I tell if food is safe by smelling or tasting it?
No. USDA says never to taste food to determine its safety. Use a thermometer and the 40°F and 2-hour rules.

### How do I avoid losing food in the first place?
Keep doors closed, keep the freezer full, use a thermometer, and run the fridge or freezer from a power station that can cover your outage length.
