**Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday on November 30.** Power stations are heavily discounted around this time, but headline percentages can mislead. This guide gives you a method that works whatever the sale prices turn out to be. We will add specific deals here only when they are live and verified.

## 🧮 The One Number to Compare: Price per Watt-Hour

Divide the price by the battery's rated capacity in watt-hours.

> **Price per Wh = price ÷ rated Wh.** Hypothetical example: a $600 unit with 1,000Wh costs $0.60 per Wh. A $900 unit with 1,500Wh also costs $0.60 per Wh.

Lower is better, **but only if the unit has the output and surge rating you need.** A cheap-per-Wh unit that cannot start your fridge is not a deal.

## ✅ Checklist Before You Buy

1. **Match output to your loads.** Check the surge rating for fridge, freezer and sump pump. See [surge vs running watts](/blog/surge-vs-running-watts/).
2. **Check battery chemistry and cycle life.** LiFePO4 units are rated for thousands of cycles. Our [LiFePO4 vs lithium-ion guide](/blog/lifepo4-vs-lithium-ion/) explains why that matters.
3. **Check the warranty and who sells it.** Buy from the manufacturer or an authorized seller. Verify the warranty length on the maker's own site.
4. **Verify the "was" price.** Compare against the maker's list price over the last few months, not just the crossed-out number.
5. **Check recharge options.** Wall charging time, solar input and car charging all matter in an outage.
6. **Read the return policy** before checkout.

## 🎯 What Each Size Is For

- **About 270Wh (e.g., Bluetti EB3A, 268Wh):** phones, router, lights, CPAP for a night or two.
- **About 1,000Wh (e.g., Jackery Explorer 1000 V2, EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus):** fridge for roughly half a day, plus small loads.
- **About 2,000Wh and up (e.g., Bluetti Elite 200 V2):** fridge plus freezer and lights for about a day, longer with solar.
