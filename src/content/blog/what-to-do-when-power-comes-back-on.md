When the lights come back on, the job is not finished. Surges, spoiled food and drained batteries are the usual problems. Work through this checklist in order.

## ✅ The Checklist

1. **Turn off or unplug big appliances you were using** when the power failed. The Red Cross warns that surges or spikes when power returns can damage equipment.
2. **Leave one light on** so you know when power is stable.
3. **Wait a few minutes**, then reconnect appliances one at a time, starting with the fridge and freezer. This avoids a pile-up of startup loads.
4. **Check the food.** Perishables should be at **40°F (4°C) or below**. The USDA says a closed refrigerator keeps food safe about 4 hours, a full freezer about 48 hours and a half-full freezer about 24 hours. Use a thermometer, and when in doubt, throw it out. See our [food-safety timeline](/blog/how-long-food-last-fridge-power-outage/).
5. **Recharge your backup power right away.** Plug your power station into the wall so it is ready for the next outage. Many units recharge to near full in one to two hours from a wall outlet, depending on the model.
6. **Reset what tripped.** Check breakers, reset clocks and thermostats, and check the sump pump and any other motors.
7. **Stay away from downed lines** and report them to the utility.

## 🔌 Generators and Carbon Monoxide

If you used a fuel-burning generator, the CDC says to **never run it inside your home or garage**, and to keep it **more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents**. Battery-powered CO alarms near sleeping areas protect you if anything goes wrong. A battery power station produces no exhaust, but it still needs a recharge.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do first when power comes back on?
Turn off or unplug large appliances you were using, leave one light on, wait a few minutes, then reconnect appliances one at a time. Surges when power returns can damage equipment.

### How do I know if my food is safe after an outage?
Perishable food should be at 40°F or below. A closed refrigerator keeps food safe about 4 hours, a full freezer about 48 hours and a half-full freezer about 24 hours, according to the USDA. When in doubt, throw it out.

### Should I unplug appliances during an outage?
Yes, for the ones that were running when the power failed. This helps prevent damage from surges when electricity is restored.

### How long does it take to recharge a power station after an outage?
It depends on the model. For example, the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus lists about 56 minutes from the wall, and the Jackery Explorer 1000 V2 about 1.6 hours.

### Is it safe to run a generator indoors?
No. The CDC says never run a generator inside your home or garage, and keep it more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents.
